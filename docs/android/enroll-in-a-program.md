# Apply to a Program

<div class="skip-to"></div>

## Apply now {: #self-enroll }

**Apply Now** (or **Enroll**) is how you join a program from its details screen. Read the overview first. You may be asked to log in or create an account. After you apply, the program appears on **My Learning**.

- Open the program ([browse on Explore](explore-programs.md#explore)).
- Read **Overview**, **Curriculum**, **Instructor**, and **Review**.
- Tap **Apply Now** or **Enroll**.

![Course details](../img/android/29-course-details.png)

- [Log in](logging-in.md#log-in-email) or [create an account](logging-in.md#create-account) if asked.
- On some programs, a **Register with a QR code/link** form opens — see [Register with a QR code/link](#cohort-enroll).

!!! note "Programs and courses"
    In this guide we say **program**. In the app you may still see **Course** — both mean the same offering.

---

## Register with a QR code/link {: #cohort-enroll }

<p class="sr-search-terms">qr qrcode qr-code scan cohort enrollment</p>

Your teacher shares a **QR code** or a **link**. Scanning or opening it starts a registration form. If you are new, this can create your account and enroll you. If you already have an account, sign in first, then submit the form.

- Scan the QR code or open the link your teacher shared.
- Fill every required field, tap **Next**, then submit.
- Wait for confirmation — you may join straight away, or a teacher may need to approve you first.
- Then [log in](logging-in.md#log-in-email). The program appears on **My Learning**.

See the form screens and extra notes in [Account & registration — Register with a QR code/link](logging-in.md#cohort-registration).

---

## Join with a registration code {: #registration-code }

Sometimes a teacher sends you a **link** or **code** instead of a QR code. Open that link, confirm you want to join, then find the program on **My Learning**.

- Open the link your teacher sent you, read the details, and confirm to join.
- Find the program on [My Learning](my-learning.md) under **Continue Learning**.

---

## Already joined — open the program {: #already-enrolled }

If you already applied, the details screen shows **View course** instead of **Apply Now**. Use that button to go straight into the program.

- Open the program details and tap **View course** (instead of **Apply Now**).
- See [Inside a Program](inside-a-program.md).

---

## If you cannot join {: #enrollment-blocked }

Some programs cannot be joined right now. The screen shows a message. Use the table below to see what to do next.

| Message | What to do |
|---------|------------|
| **Course is full** | Ask your teacher or use Help & Support in the app |
| **Enrollment closed** | Watch for a later offering |
| **Invitation only** | Ask your teacher |
| **Prerequisite required** | Complete the required program first |
