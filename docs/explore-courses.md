## Browse programs {: #browse-courses }

The home page and program list show all programs you can join. Use search and filters to find a topic, then open a card to read more and apply.

- Open [https://mycommunity.vigyanshaala.com/](https://mycommunity.vigyanshaala.com/){ target="_blank" } or the [program list](https://apps.mycommunity.vigyanshaala.com/public/courses){ target="_blank" }.
- Use **Search**, **Categories**, **Tags**, **Topics**, and **Sort** to find a program.
- Click **View Program** on a card.

!!! note "Programs and courses"
    In this guide we say **program**. On the site you may still see **Course** — both mean the same offering.

## Read program details {: #course-about }

The details page explains what you will learn, who teaches, and what other learners said. Read it before you click **Apply Now**.

On the details page, use these tabs:

| Tab | What it is for |
|-----|----------------|
| **Overview** | What the program is about |
| **Course Content** | Lessons and topics |
| **Trainer** | Who teaches or mentors |
| **Reviews** | What other learners said |

- Click the **heart** to save to your [wishlist](my-dashboard.md#my-programs).
- Click **Apply Now** when ready — see [Apply to a Program](enroll-in-a-course.md).

![Program details with Apply Now](img/08-course-about.png)

---

## Save to wishlist {: #wishlist }

Wishlist is a save list for programs you might join later. Saving does not enroll you — you still need to click **Apply Now** when you are ready.

- Open a program and click the **heart** on the right-hand card.
- Find saved programs on [My Dashboard](my-dashboard.md#my-programs) → **Wishlist** tab.
- Click the heart again if you want to remove it from the list.
